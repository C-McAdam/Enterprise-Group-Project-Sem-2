Also empty cells and those that contained null values were removed. Extra spaces were removed using the (=TRIM) function on excel to make sure that all extra spaces were removed to avoid getting biased analysis results. Outliers were checked and fixed. Machine Learning
In this project, a simple machine learning concept was used to understand how study patterns affect student performance.
Linear regression was used to predict the current semester CGPA based on total study hours. A scatter plot with a regression line showed a positive relationship, meaning that students who studied more hours generally had higher CGPA. The regression equation also showed that CGPA increases as study hours increase, confirming that study time is an important factor in academic performance.
Logistic regression was used to predict whether a student would pass or fail based on their study hours. Students with a CGPA of 8.0 or higher were classified as pass. The logistic regression graph showed an S-shaped curve, indicating that the probability of passing increases as total study hours increase.
Overall, these models demonstrated that study habits are strongly related to academic success and show how machine learning can be used to predict student performance.

Limitations and Ethics
It is of paramount significance to note that there are several limitations to this analysis. Firstly, the dataset may only represent a small group of students and may not reflect all students. This means the results cannot be applied to all educational settings. Also some data may be missing or incomplete. Even after cleaning, missing values can affect the accuracy of statistical analysis and predictions. Lastly, academic performance is influenced by many factors, such as motivation, teaching quality, mental health, and personal circumstances. Study time alone does not fully explain student performance.
There are also ethical considerations when analyzing student data. Student information must be kept confidential and used responsibly. Personal identities should not be shared, and data should only be used for educational purposes.

Conclusion
To conclude, this project collected a dataset containing information about students’ academic performance and study patterns. The data was cleaned by removing duplicate records, handling missing values, and ensuring correct formats to ensure accuracy. Descriptive statistical analysis was performed to calculate measures such as mean, median, minimum, maximum, and standard deviation. These statistics helped summarize student performance and study behavior.
Visual analysis using graphs showed clear relationships between study time, screen time, and academic performance. Students who studied more hours generally achieved better academic results, while excessive screen time appeared to have a negative effect.
A basic machine learning concept was introduced to demonstrate how student performance could be predicted based on study patterns. This showed how data analysis can be used to support educational decision-making.
