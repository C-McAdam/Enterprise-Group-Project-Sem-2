GPA vs Hours Studied Graph

X = Hours Studied, Y = Current GPA



What we predicted

We predicted the Linear Regression of students' hours studied and their resulting GPA.


What features were used

A linear regression script was used originally in python, which was then implemented into this website using Charts.js

A line graph was used to show all the data and show where it clusters


What this tells us

We can read from this graph that the students who studied from 2-4 hours performed the worst out of the group, with 5.5 to 6.7 GPA,

whereas students who studied from 7-9 hours were the most variable in their GPA, but were the only ones to crack 9.7 GPA.
